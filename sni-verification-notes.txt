Russian REALITY SNI candidates
Exported: 2026-10-08T11:06:39+00:00

Selected: 7961 unique hostnames
Checked: 34874 unique hostnames
Source dataset: 20499 .ru domains, plus earlier candidates

Selection requirements:
- Public IPv4 HTTPS service on TCP port 443.
- Successful TLS 1.3 handshake with normal certificate-chain and hostname verification.
- HTTP/2 selected through ALPN (h2).
- HTTP 200 from a separate HTTP/1.1 GET, after at most two HTTPS redirects
  within the same hostname. Captcha/challenge redirect paths are not followed.
- Separate verified TLS 1.3 handshake using X25519 only.

Scope and interpretation:
- Every base hostname in the downloaded .ru dataset was considered.
- A www hostname is additionally tried when the base hostname fails selection.
- Previous selected hostnames are also checked, retaining discovered subdomains
  only when they pass the current measurements.
- The initial snapshot included extra redirect discovery and transient retries;
  scheduled refreshes perform fresh checks of source domains and existing candidates.
- At most two resolved public IPv4 addresses are tried per hostname.
- TCP connection failures for a shared IP are cached for up to 10 minutes;
  names skipped for that reason are excluded from the selected list.
- TLS/HTTP behavior can differ by address, time, location, or client fingerprint.
- Verification origin: GitHub Actions ubuntu-latest hosted runner. Results depend on this network location.
- A full authenticated VLESS/REALITY connection was not tested.
- A .ru suffix does not establish Russian ownership, hosting, or ISP allowlisting.
- HTTP/2 application traffic, site content/reputation, OCSP stapling, and
  post-quantum key exchange were not assessed.
- Existing candidates are first; additions follow the dataset's relative rank.
- Different hostnames may share an IP or infrastructure.
